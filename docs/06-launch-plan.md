# 06 — Launch plan

## Why the calendar decides everything

This product has one natural sales window and you are sitting right in front of it.

```
AUG ──── SEP ──── OCT ──── NOV ──── DEC ──── JAN ──── FEB ──── MAR ──── APR
 │        │        │        │                 │                 │        │
 build   launch  season   winter          peak anxiety      inspection  season
 quietly  loud    closes   work                                season     opens
                          begins        "am I ready?"      "10 days notice"
                                                                    ▲
                                            ─────────────── this is your Black Friday
```

Starting in **August 2026** is close to ideal: you have six weeks to build before the
season closes, and hosts hit peak compliance anxiety in January–February with the
inspection regime live and the €5,000 first fine already being reported in host groups.

**Do not spend the winter building.** Build now, sell in winter.

---

## Days 1–14 · Build (mid-to-late August)

Follow `04-build-guide-nocode.md`. Ship the app before you write a word of marketing.

**Definition of done:** you can sign up with a fresh email, add a property, see a score
and a fine-exposure number, upload one certificate, pay €9, and receive a reminder email
the next morning. If any step in that chain is manual, it is not done.

Run it against **your own or a friend's property first.** If you do not personally know a
Greek host, that is the actual first problem to solve — go find five before you build
anything.

---

## Days 15–30 · Ten design partners (early September)

**Goal: 10 hosts using it, 0 paying.** Not a typo. You are buying accuracy, not revenue.

- Post in Greek host Facebook groups — *Airbnb Ελλάδα*, *Βραχυχρόνια Μίσθωση Ελλάδα*, and
  regional groups for Crete, Paros, Naxos, Athens. **Do not pitch.** Post the free
  compliance checklist as a PDF, ask what you got wrong, and answer every comment.
- DM 30 co-hosts and small property managers found via Airbnb listings and local
  Facebook. Offer free lifetime Manager tier in exchange for a 30-minute call.
- Ask one question in every call: **"What did you get fined for, or nearly fined for?"**
  The answers rewrite your rule set and give you the copy for the entire launch.

**Ship whatever they tell you is wrong.** At this stage your rule set is compiled from
secondary sources — these ten people are how it becomes trustworthy.

**Non-negotiable:** get a Greek accountant to review `compliance-rules.json` this month.
Pay them. This is the single highest-leverage €150 in the plan.

---

## Days 31–60 · Content and the free tool (late September – October)

Season is closing. Hosts are shutting up shop and turning to admin. Turn the free checker
into a distribution engine.

**Publish, in Greek first with English versions:**

1. *"Οι 16 υποχρεώσεις κάθε βραχυχρόνιας μίσθωσης το 2026"* — the definitive checklist
2. *"Πρόστιμο €5.000: τι ελέγχουν στην επιθεώρηση"* — what inspectors actually check
3. *"Τι να κάνεις τον χειμώνα για να είσαι έτοιμος τον Απρίλιο"* — the winter work plan
4. *"ΤΑΚ 2026: πόσο χρεώνεις και πότε το δηλώνεις"* — the fee, with the calculator embedded

Each one ends in the free compliance check. Each check ends in an email capture.

**Distribution that actually works for this market, ranked:**

| Channel | Why it works here |
|---|---|
| Greek Facebook host groups | Where this audience genuinely lives. Highest intent, zero cost |
| Accountants (λογιστές) | They have 50 rental clients each. Offer a free Manager account and a referral fee. **The best channel nobody uses** |
| Property managers | Multiply reach — one manager brings 15 properties |
| r/greece, expat forums | Reaches the foreign-owner segment |
| Local Facebook groups per island | Chania, Rethymno, Paros, Naxos, Corfu — small, warm, and unsaturated |

The accountant channel deserves the most effort. A Greek accountant with 50 STR clients
who recommends Fakelos is worth more than any ad budget, and they will do it because it
removes work from their desk in exactly the season when clients call them with questions
they cannot bill for.

**Target by day 60:** 400 free users, 25 paying, €270 MRR.

---

## Days 61–90 · The winter push (November)

The season is closed. Your customers have time, no income, and a to-do list. This is when
you sell.

**The core campaign — "Έτοιμος μέχρι τον Απρίλιο" / "Ready by April":**

Every host who ran the free check gets a 6-email sequence:
1. Your score, and the three things missing
2. What a €5,000 fine actually costs you (in nights let)
3. Your personalised winter work plan, with dates
4. The item with the longest lead time — book your electrician now
5. Annual plan offer: €89 for the year, framed as "cheaper than one pest control visit and
   a fine"
6. Last call before December

**Push annual hard this month.** November is the one moment a host is thinking in full-year
terms, and annual billing is the main defence against the summer churn described in
`05-monetization.md`.

**Also in November:**
- Launch the Manager tier properly, with a portfolio dashboard demo video
- Set up the insurance referral partnership — hosts are buying policies right now
- Ask your ten design partners for testimonials. Use real names and real islands

**Target by day 90:** 700 free users, 55 paying, 60% on annual, ~€600 MRR.

---

## Beyond 90 days

**December–February — peak season for you.** Inspection anxiety is highest. Publish every
enforcement story you can find. Run one modest Meta campaign targeting Greek property
owners; this is the only month the ad economics clearly work.

**March — the deadline month.** "Your property must be ready before your first booking."
Highest-urgency messaging of the year.

**April–September — the quiet half.** Do not panic when growth flattens; this is the
seasonal shape, not failure. Use it to: ship ΤΑΚ tracking properly (problem #3), build the
caretaking module (problem #2), deepen the accountant channel, and keep the rule set
current.

---

## What would tell you this is not working

Set these tripwires now, while you are still objective:

- **Fewer than 30% of free-check completions give you an email.** The score isn't scary
  enough or the report isn't valuable enough. Fix the report.
- **Fewer than 5% of email captures convert to paid within 60 days.** Free tier is giving
  away the relief, not just the diagnosis. Move the gate.
- **Host churn above 8% monthly by month 6.** Stop selling monthly. Annual only.
- **Nobody in the Facebook groups engages with the free checklist.** The problem is not
  felt as urgently as the fine schedule suggests. This is the one that would make me stop
  — and it is cheap to test in week three, before you have built anything expensive.

That last tripwire is the important one. Everything in this plan is downstream of the
assumption that Greek hosts are genuinely anxious about Law 5170/2025. **Test that in
September with a PDF and a Facebook post, before you spend a winter on it.**
